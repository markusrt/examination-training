## Task 1 – Reported Statements (6 BE)

Rewrite the following sentences in reported speech. Use the introductory words given in brackets.

**a)** "I am visiting my grandparents this weekend." (Tom said ...) [2 BE]

**b)** "We have already finished our homework." (The students told their teacher ...) [2 BE]

**c)** "I will call you tomorrow." (Lisa told her friend ...) [2 BE]

---

## Task 2 – Reported Questions (6 BE)

Turn the following direct questions into reported questions.

**a)** "Are you coming to the party on Saturday?" (Jake asked Emma ...) [2 BE]

**b)** "Where did you buy that jacket?" (Mia asked her brother ...) [2 BE]

**c)** "Have you ever been to London?" (The teacher asked the class ...) [2 BE]

---

## Task 3 – Reported Commands and Requests (4 BE)

Rewrite the following sentences as reported commands or requests.

**a)** "Close the window, please." (Mrs Miller asked the students ...) [2 BE]

**b)** "Don't use your phones during the lesson!" (The teacher told the class ...) [2 BE]

---

## Task 4 – Mixed: Find and Correct the Mistakes (4 BE)

Each sentence contains **one** mistake in the reported speech. Find the mistake, underline it and write the correct version.

**a)** Tom said that he is tired after the long trip. [1 BE]

**b)** She asked me where do I live. [1 BE]

**c)** The coach told the players don't give up. [1 BE]

**d)** He said that he will meet us the following day. [1 BE]
