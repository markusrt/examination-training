# Musterlösung: Englisch-Stegreifaufgabe – Bayern 9. Klasse

> The following answers show the expected solutions. Minor variations in wording are acceptable as long as the reported speech rules are applied correctly.

---

## Task 1 – Reported Statements (6 BE)

### a) [2 BE]

"I am visiting my grandparents this weekend."

**Tom said (that) he was visiting his grandparents that weekend.**

Backshift: am visiting → was visiting
Changes: I → he, this weekend → that weekend

### b) [2 BE]

"We have already finished our homework."

**The students told their teacher (that) they had already finished their homework.**

Backshift: have finished → had finished
Changes: we → they, our → their

### c) [2 BE]

"I will call you tomorrow."

**Lisa told her friend (that) she would call her/him the next day.**

Backshift: will call → would call
Changes: I → she, you → her/him, tomorrow → the next day (/ the following day)

## Task 2 – Reported Questions (6 BE)

### a) [2 BE]

"Are you coming to the party on Saturday?"

**Jake asked Emma if/whether she was coming to the party on Saturday.**

Yes/No question → if/whether + statement word order
Backshift: are coming → was coming
Changes: you → she

### b) [2 BE]

"Where did you buy that jacket?"

**Mia asked her brother where he had bought that jacket.**

Wh-question → wh-word + statement word order (no auxiliary "did")
Backshift: did buy (bought) → had bought
Changes: you → he

### c) [2 BE]

"Have you ever been to London?"

**The teacher asked the class if/whether they had ever been to London.**

Yes/No question → if/whether + statement word order
Backshift: have been → had been
Changes: you → they

## Task 3 – Reported Commands and Requests (4 BE)

### a) [2 BE]

"Close the window, please."

**Mrs Miller asked the students to close the window.**

Request (please) → asked + to-infinitive

### b) [2 BE]

"Don't use your phones during the lesson!"

**The teacher told the class not to use their phones during the lesson.**

Negative command → told + not to + infinitive
Changes: your → their

## Task 4 – Mixed: Find and Correct the Mistakes (4 BE)

### a) [1 BE]

~~is~~ → **was**

Tom said that he **was** tired after the long trip.

(Backshift: is → was)

### b) [1 BE]

~~do I live~~ → **I lived**

She asked me where **I lived**.

(Reported question requires statement word order + backshift: live → lived)

### c) [1 BE]

~~don't give up~~ → **not to give up**

The coach told the players **not to give up**.

(Reported command: told + not to + infinitive)

### d) [1 BE]

~~will~~ → **would**

He said that he **would** meet us the following day.

(Backshift: will → would)
