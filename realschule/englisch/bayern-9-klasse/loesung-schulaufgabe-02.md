# Musterlösung: Englisch-Schulaufgabe – Bayern 9. Klasse

> The following answers show the expected solutions. Wording in Parts 1 and 3 may vary – any answer that is content-correct, grammatically sound and uses the student's own words is accepted.

---

## Part 1 – Reading Comprehension (15 BE)

### a) [3 BE]

*SecondLife Online* is a small start-up company (ll. 3–4). It buys used clothes from private customers and then sells them again on its website to people in Germany, Ireland and the UK (ll. 4, 14). In other words, it is an online shop for second-hand clothing.

*Bewertung:* 1 BE Art des Unternehmens (Start-up / Online-Shop), 1 BE Einkauf von Gebrauchtkleidung, 1 BE Wiederverkauf über das Internet.

### b) [3 BE]

When clothes arrive, the team first checks their condition (ll. 9–10). If an item is still in good condition, they calculate a price for it and put it online on their website (ll. 10–11). Items that are too old or broken are not sold but given to a charity shop in town (ll. 11–12).

*Bewertung:* 1 BE Prüfung der Ware, 1 BE Verkauf bei guter Qualität, 1 BE Spende bei schlechter Qualität.

### c) [3 BE]

During his week, Tom has to:

- take photos of the clothes (l. 13)
- write short product descriptions in English for the website (ll. 13–14)
- answer customer messages, e.g. the complaint about the jeans (ll. 19–20)

*Bewertung:* je 1 BE pro korrekt genannter Aufgabe.

### d) [4 BE]

The problem on Wednesday is that a customer from Manchester is not satisfied with her order: the jeans she has bought are too small for her, so she writes a complaint and asks for her money back (ll. 18–19).

Tom solves the problem by writing a polite reply in which he offers her two possibilities: either she gets a **refund** or she can choose a different pair of jeans for the same price (ll. 19–21). The customer chooses the second option and is so happy that she even writes a positive online comment about the shop.

*Bewertung:* 2 BE Problem (unzufriedene Kundin, zu kleine Jeans, Wunsch nach Rückerstattung), 2 BE Lösung (höfliche Antwort, zwei Optionen, positives Ergebnis).

### e) [2 BE]

(1) **False.** *SecondLife Online* also delivers to Ireland and the UK, not only to Germany (ll. 14–15). [1 BE]

(2) **True.** Sarah told Tom not to worry because every customer makes spelling mistakes (ll. 16–17). [1 BE]

*Bewertung:* 0,5 BE für true/false-Entscheidung, 0,5 BE für korrekte Berichtigung bzw. Begründung – nur volle BE bei beidem.

## Part 2 – Reported Speech (10 BE)

### a) [2 BE]

"I am very happy with your work here."

**Mrs Khan told Tom (that) she was very happy with his work there.**

- Backshift: *am* → *was*
- Pronoun: *I* → *she*; *your* → *his*
- Place: *here* → *there*

### b) [2 BE]

"We sold more than fifty jackets last week."

**Sarah explained (that) they had sold more than fifty jackets the week before / the previous week.**

- Backshift: *sold* (Simple Past) → *had sold* (Past Perfect)
- Pronoun: *we* → *they*
- Time: *last week* → *the week before / the previous week*

### c) [2 BE]

"When will I get my refund?"

**The customer wanted to know when she would get her refund.**

- Wh-question → wh-word + statement word order (no question mark)
- Backshift: *will get* → *would get*
- Pronoun: *I* → *she*; *my* → *her*

### d) [2 BE]

"Have you already taken the photos for the new shoes?"

**Mrs Khan asked Tom if/whether he had already taken the photos for the new shoes.**

- Yes/no question → *if/whether* + statement word order
- Backshift: *have taken* (Present Perfect) → *had taken* (Past Perfect)
- Pronoun: *you* → *he*

### e) [2 BE]

"Don't forget to answer the customer's email today!"

**Sarah reminded Tom not to forget to answer the customer's email that day.**

- Negative command → *not to* + infinitive
- Time: *today* → *that day*

*Bewertung pro Teilaufgabe:* 1 BE für korrekte Satzkonstruktion / Backshift, 1 BE für korrekte Pronomen- und Zeit-/Ortsanpassung.

## Part 3 – Text Production: Inquiry (20 BE)

### Mustertext (ca. 145 Wörter)

> Dear Ms O'Brien,
>
> My name is Lukas Bauer and I am a 15-year-old student at the *Städtische Realschule* in Regensburg, Germany. I am writing to enquire about the possibility of working as a junior at GreenBag Trading Ltd. for two weeks during the summer holidays in August.
>
> I found your advertisement on the internet and I am very interested in your company because I would like to learn more about international online trade and improve my English at the same time.
>
> I would be very grateful if you could answer the following questions:
>
> - What kind of tasks would I be given in the office and the warehouse?
> - What are the usual working hours, and would there be any pay or expenses?
> - Could you recommend a host family or affordable accommodation in Dublin?
>
> At school, I study English, business and IT. I have good computer skills, my English is at level B1 and I have already helped my parents to sell items online.
>
> I look forward to hearing from you soon.
>
> Yours sincerely,
> Lukas Bauer

### Bewertung der Inquiry (20 BE)

| Bereich | Kriterien | BE |
|---|---|---|
| **Inhalt (8 BE)** | Vorstellung (Name, Alter, Schule) | 1 |
|  | Anlass / Bitte um Junior-Praktikum, Zeitraum genannt | 2 |
|  | Mindestens 3 konkrete Fragen zum Praktikum | 3 |
|  | Eigene Qualifikationen / Angebot des Bewerbers | 1 |
|  | Bitte um Antwort | 1 |
| **Sprache (8 BE)** | Grammatik (Tempora, Satzbau, höfliche Frageformen) | 4 |
|  | Wortschatz (formell, abwechslungsreich, *buying and selling*) | 3 |
|  | Rechtschreibung & Zeichensetzung | 1 |
| **Aufbau & Form (4 BE)** | Korrekte Anrede (*Dear Ms O'Brien,*) | 1 |
|  | Sinnvolle Absatzgliederung | 1 |
|  | Höfliche Grußformel (*Yours sincerely, …*) | 1 |
|  | Formaler Stil (keine Kurzformen, Wortzahl 120–150) | 1 |
| **Gesamt** |  | **20** |

> Hinweis zur Sprachbewertung: Bei deutlich weniger als 120 oder deutlich mehr als 150 Wörtern erfolgt ein Abzug von bis zu 2 BE im Bereich „Aufbau & Form". Werden Kurzformen (z. B. *I'm*, *don't*) durchgängig verwendet, gibt es ebenfalls Abzug im Stilbereich.
