# Musterlösung: Englisch-Schulaufgabe – Bayern 9. Klasse

> The following answers show the expected solutions. Wording in Parts 1 and 3 may vary – any answer that is content-correct, grammatically sound and uses the student's own words is accepted.

---

## Part 1 – Reading Comprehension (15 BE)

### a) [3 BE]

Lisa goes to a *Wirtschaftsschule* in Munich (l. 1). What is special about her placement is that she does not work at a local company in Munich like most of her classmates, but travels abroad to a company in Manchester, England (ll. 2–6).

*Bewertung:* 1 BE Schultyp/Ort, 2 BE Vergleich mit Mitschülern.

### b) [3 BE]

Lisa wanted to do her placement in Manchester because she wanted to **improve her English** (l. 4). Working in an English-speaking country gives her the chance to use the language every day with native speakers, which is not possible at a German company in Munich.

*Bewertung:* 1 BE Sprachverbesserung (Textbeleg), 2 BE eigenständige Begründung.

### c) [3 BE]

During her placement, Lisa has to:

- answer simple emails from German customers (l. 10)
- file invoices (l. 10)
- help in the warehouse when new goods arrive (ll. 10–11)

*Bewertung:* je 1 BE pro korrekt genannter Aufgabe.

### d) [4 BE]

At the beginning, Lisa feels **excited but also nervous** (l. 7) because she has never travelled to England on her own before. In addition, she only understands about half of what her colleagues say at lunch (ll. 12–13), which is frustrating for her.

By the end of the two weeks, she feels **tired but happy** (l. 15). Her English has improved, she has learned a lot of new business vocabulary and she has gained self-confidence – she even writes that she now knows she "can do it" (l. 16). The change happens because she gets used to the language and the new environment after a few days.

*Bewertung:* 1 BE Anfang, 1 BE Ende, 2 BE Begründung/Erklärung der Veränderung.

### e) [2 BE]

(1) **False.** Most of Lisa's classmates apply to local shops, banks or offices in Munich, not abroad (ll. 2–3). [1 BE]

(2) **False.** Lisa only helps in the warehouse when new goods arrive; her main tasks are office work like answering emails and filing invoices (ll. 9–11). [1 BE]

*Bewertung:* 0,5 BE für true/false-Entscheidung, 0,5 BE für korrekte Berichtigung – nur volle BE bei beidem.

## Part 2 – Reported Speech (10 BE)

### a) [2 BE]

"I am really enjoying my placement here."

**Lisa told Mrs Bennett (that) she was really enjoying her placement there.**

- Backshift: *am enjoying* → *was enjoying*
- Pronoun: *I* → *she*; *my* → *her*
- Place: *here* → *there*

### b) [2 BE]

"We received a new order from Berlin yesterday."

**Mrs Bennett explained (that) they had received a new order from Berlin the day before / the previous day.**

- Backshift: *received* (Simple Past) → *had received* (Past Perfect)
- Pronoun: *we* → *they*
- Time: *yesterday* → *the day before / the previous day*

### c) [2 BE]

"When will I get my next task?"

**Lisa wanted to know when she would get her next task.**

- Wh-question → wh-word + statement word order (no question mark)
- Backshift: *will get* → *would get*
- Pronoun: *I* → *she*; *my* → *her*

### d) [2 BE]

"Have you ever worked in an office before?"

**The colleague asked Lisa if/whether she had ever worked in an office before.**

- Yes/no question → *if/whether* + statement word order
- Backshift: *have worked* (Present Perfect) → *had worked* (Past Perfect)
- Pronoun: *you* → *she*

### e) [2 BE]

"Don't forget to send the invoices today!"

**Mrs Bennett reminded Lisa not to forget to send the invoices that day.**

- Negative command → *not to* + infinitive
- Time: *today* → *that day*

*Bewertung pro Teilaufgabe:* 1 BE für korrekte Satzkonstruktion / Backshift, 1 BE für korrekte Pronomen- und Zeit-/Ortsanpassung.

## Part 3 – Text Production: Inquiry (20 BE)

### Mustertext (ca. 145 Wörter)

> Dear Mr Carter,
>
> My name is Anna Müller and I am a 15-year-old student at the *Städtische Wirtschaftsschule* in Munich, Germany. I am writing to enquire about the possibility of doing a two-week work placement at Greenway Trading Ltd. in October this year.
>
> I found your advertisement on the internet and I am very interested in your company because I would like to gain practical experience in international trade and improve my English at the same time.
>
> I would be very grateful if you could answer the following questions:
>
> - What kind of tasks would I be given during my placement?
> - What are the usual working hours, and would I have to work on Saturdays?
> - Could you recommend a host family or affordable accommodation in Brighton?
>
> At school, I study business administration, accounting and English. I also have good computer skills (Word, Excel) and my English is at level B1.
>
> I look forward to hearing from you soon.
>
> Yours sincerely,
> Anna Müller

### Bewertung der Inquiry (20 BE)

| Bereich | Kriterien | BE |
|---|---|---|
| **Inhalt (8 BE)** | Vorstellung (Name, Alter, Schule) | 1 |
|  | Anlass / Bitte um Praktikum, Zeitraum genannt | 2 |
|  | Mindestens 3 konkrete Fragen zum Praktikum | 3 |
|  | Eigene Qualifikationen / Angebot des Bewerbers | 1 |
|  | Bitte um Antwort | 1 |
| **Sprache (8 BE)** | Grammatik (Tempora, Satzbau, höfliche Frageformen) | 4 |
|  | Wortschatz (formell, abwechslungsreich, themenspezifisch) | 3 |
|  | Rechtschreibung & Zeichensetzung | 1 |
| **Aufbau & Form (4 BE)** | Korrekte Anrede (*Dear Mr Carter,*) | 1 |
|  | Sinnvolle Absatzgliederung | 1 |
|  | Höfliche Grußformel (*Yours sincerely, …*) | 1 |
|  | Formaler Stil (keine Kurzformen, Wortzahl 120–150) | 1 |
| **Gesamt** |  | **20** |

> Hinweis zur Sprachbewertung: Bei deutlich weniger als 120 oder deutlich mehr als 150 Wörtern erfolgt ein Abzug von bis zu 2 BE im Bereich „Aufbau & Form". Werden Kurzformen (z. B. *I'm*, *don't*) durchgängig verwendet, gibt es ebenfalls Abzug im Stilbereich.
