## Part 1 – Reading Comprehension (15 BE)

Read the following text carefully. Then answer the questions below in **complete English sentences**. Use your **own words** as far as possible – do not just copy whole sentences from the text.

---

**My week as a junior at *SecondLife Online***

| | |
|---:|:---|
|  1 | Tom is 15 years old and goes to a *Realschule* in Nuremberg. As part of his school's |
|  2 | career week, every student in Year 9 has to spend five days as a "junior" at a real |
|  3 | company. Tom did not want to work in a normal shop, so he applied to *SecondLife |
|  4 | Online*, a small start-up that buys used clothes from private customers and sells |
|  5 | them again on the internet. The owner, Mrs Khan, accepted him by email two weeks |

| 11 | price is calculated and the item is listed on the website. Items that are too old or |
| 12 | broken are given to a charity shop in town. |
| 13 | Tom's main task was to take photos of the clothes and to write short product |
| 14 | descriptions in English, because *SecondLife Online* also delivers to Ireland and |
| 15 | the UK. At first he was afraid of writing wrong sentences, but his colleague Sarah |
| 16 | helped him a lot. "Don't worry," she said on the second day, "every customer makes |
| 17 | spelling mistakes – just keep it short and friendly." |
| 18 | On Wednesday a problem came up: a customer from Manchester complained that the |
| 19 | jeans she had bought were too small and asked for a refund. Tom had to write a |
| 20 | polite answer and offer her either her money back or a different pair for the same |
| 21 | price. The customer chose the second option and even left a positive comment online. |
| 22 | At the end of the week, Mrs Khan thanked Tom: "You worked really hard, and your |

---

**a)** What kind of company is *SecondLife Online* and what does it do? Answer in your own words. [3 BE]

**b)** Explain how the team decides what happens with the clothes that customers send in. [3 BE]

**c)** Name **three** different tasks Tom has to do during his week as a junior. [3 BE]

**d)** Describe the problem on Wednesday and explain how Tom solved it. [4 BE]

**e)** Decide whether the following statements are **true** or **false**. Correct the false ones with a short sentence (in English). [2 BE]

  - (1) *SecondLife Online* only sells its products in Germany.
  - (2) Sarah told Tom that small spelling mistakes were not a big problem.

## Part 2 – Reported Speech (10 BE)

After his week at *SecondLife Online*, Tom tells his classmates what people said. Rewrite each sentence in **reported speech**. Pay attention to backshift of tenses, pronouns and time/place expressions.

**a)** Mrs Khan said: "I am very happy with your work here." (Mrs Khan told Tom ...) [2 BE]

**b)** Sarah said: "We sold more than fifty jackets last week." (Sarah explained ...) [2 BE]

**c)** A customer asked: "When will I get my refund?" (The customer wanted to know ...) [2 BE]

**d)** Mrs Khan asked Tom: "Have you already taken the photos for the new shoes?" (Mrs Khan asked Tom ...) [2 BE]

**e)** Sarah said to Tom: "Don't forget to answer the customer's email today!" (Sarah reminded Tom ...) [2 BE]

## Part 3 – Text Production: Inquiry (20 BE)

Imagine **you** are a student at a *Realschule* in Bavaria. You would like to spend two weeks as a **junior** at a small company in **Dublin (Ireland)** during the summer holidays. You found the following advertisement on the internet:

> **GreenBag Trading Ltd., Dublin**
> *Online shop for fair-trade products – buying from European suppliers, selling to customers all over Ireland and the UK.*
> From time to time we welcome students from abroad as junior helpers in our office and warehouse.
> Contact: Ms Aoife O'Brien, jobs@greenbag-trading.ie

Write a **formal email of inquiry** to Ms O'Brien (about **120–150 words**). Include the following points:

- a suitable greeting and a short introduction (who you are, your school, your age)
- the reason why you are writing (junior placement, two weeks, time period)
- ask **at least three concrete questions** about the placement (e.g. tasks, working hours, accommodation, costs, language requirements)
- mention what you can already offer (school subjects, computer skills, level of English, experience with buying / selling, …)
- ask for a reply and finish with a polite closing formula

> 💡 **Remember:** Use **formal language** (no contractions like *I'm* or *don't*), correct email layout (greeting, paragraphs, closing) and **polite question forms** ("Could you please tell me …", "I would like to know whether …").
